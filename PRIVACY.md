# Draw by Starlight privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

Draw by Starlight has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads, and it does not use the
network. Its developer receives nothing from the app: not where you are, what you draw or anything
about how you use it.

The story of each constellation is quoted from Wikipedia and comes bundled with the app, so
reading it needs no connection. Under each story is a link to the Wikipedia article; tapping it
opens Safari, outside the app, and only then does Wikipedia see a request, from Safari, as for
any web page you visit.

## What stays on your iPhone

Everything the app knows is computed or stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
Its data is included in your iPhone's own backups like any app's data.

## Permissions

Draw by Starlight asks for no permission. It does not use your location: it guesses the part of
the world you are in from the time zone your iPhone is set to, on the device, and keeps nothing
of it.

## Children

Draw by Starlight collects nothing from anyone.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published here, in the app's public repository, with the date above.
